Montiva Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21/10/2026

Summary

Montiva is a video and text editor for Android. Your projects, videos and texts stay on your device. We do not run accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Information the app accesses

  • Videos, audio and text files you select with the Android system picker or share to Montiva. They are used only to edit and export your projects.
  • Exported videos are saved to Movies › Montiva on your device.
  • Projects, texts and settings are stored in Montiva's private app storage.

Internet use

Montiva connects to the internet only when you ask it to download a video from a link you enter (for example Internet Archive or Wikimedia Commons), and when Google Play processes purchases. These services receive the network requests needed to deliver the content.

Purchases

Montiva Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play under Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Montiva receives only confirmation of your purchase; we never see your payment details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you choose to share an exported video or text with another app.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete projects and texts in the app at any time. Uninstalling Montiva removes all app data. Exported videos in Movies › Montiva remain yours to keep or delete.

Children

Montiva is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
