Pocket Video Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This policy describes how Pocket Video (Android package com.kanbo.pocket_video), developed by Ahmad Kanbour, handles information when you look up videos, download permitted content, play saved files, export files, or contact support.

1. Information handled by the app

Pocket Video does not require an account. Its current version has no advertising, analytics SDK, subscription, or developer-operated backend. It does not send your saved video library to the developer.

  • Video links: The app processes the link you enter to identify the requested video. If you tap Paste, it reads the clipboard text to fill the link field; it does not continuously monitor your clipboard.
  • Network requests: To retrieve video information, thumbnails, and media, the app contacts YouTube and Google-operated media servers. These services receive the requested video or resource identifiers, your IP address, and request headers. They may process or retain this information under their own policies.
  • Saved files: Downloaded video/audio, temporary transfer files, and library metadata are processed on your device. Library metadata includes the video title, video ID, quality, file size, and date saved.
  • Support: If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as your message, screenshots, or diagnostic details. We use this information to respond and troubleshoot.

2. Purposes and sharing

Information is used to provide the lookup, download, playback, export, and support features you request. The app does not sell your personal information or send it to advertising partners.

YouTube/Google receive the network information described above as part of fulfilling your requests. If you export a video, you select its destination using Android's document picker. A destination may be on your device or provided by a cloud-storage service; that service then handles the exported file under its own policy. Support email is processed by our email provider, Gmail/Google.

3. Permissions and device access

The app uses Internet access for video lookups, thumbnails, and downloads. It stores its private library in app-specific storage and uses Android's document picker to write exported files to a destination you select. Its current version does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or the full contents of your shared storage.

4. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Saved videos and metadata remain in the private library until you delete them in the app or remove the app's data. Uninstalling removes the app's private library. Exported copies are separate and must be deleted from their selected destination; deleting an item in Pocket Video does not delete exported copies.

Temporary download files are removed after completion, cancellation, or failure where possible. After interruption, remaining partial downloads are cleaned up when the app next starts successfully. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address your request or meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you sent to support. We cannot delete data independently retained by Google or another destination provider; contact that provider for its available controls.

5. Security and diagnostics

The app uses HTTPS for its connections to YouTube/Google and Android's app-specific storage for its private library. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Development/debug builds may write diagnostic errors to local device logs; the app does not automatically upload these logs to the developer.

Google Play and Android may separately process installation, device, crash, or diagnostic information according to their settings and policies, and may make reports available to developers. Pocket Video does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

6. Third-party services and this webpage

YouTube/Google, Google Play, and any export destination operate under their own privacy policies. This privacy-policy webpage is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives technical information, including your IP address, when you visit it. This page contains no added analytics, tracking scripts, external fonts, or contact form.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub General Privacy Statement

Third-party services may process information outside your country. Their policies explain their retention practices, international processing, and available privacy controls.

7. Children's privacy

The app has no age-verification or account system. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can address the request and remove that information where appropriate.
